How long does it take to read The Great Gatsby?

The Great Gatsby by F. Scott Fitzgerald has about 48,121 words in the edition included in Lince. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, that is about 3 h 22 min.

Times are pure reading, without breaks. At 300 words per page, this edition would be about 160 pages.
