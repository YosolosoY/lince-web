How long does it take to read Meditations?

Meditations by Marcus Aurelius has about 46,035 words in the edition included in Lince. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, that is about 3 h 13 min.

Reading time at different speeds

SpeedTime
Slow reader (150 wpm)5 h 07 min
Average (238 wpm)3 h 13 min
Fast (300 wpm)2 h 33 min
Very fast (400 wpm)1 h 55 min

Times are pure reading, without breaks. At 300 words per page, this edition would be about 153 pages.
