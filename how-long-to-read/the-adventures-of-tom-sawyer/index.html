How long does it take to read The Adventures of Tom Sawyer?

The Adventures of Tom Sawyer by Mark Twain has about 69,948 words in the edition included in Lince. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, that is about 4 h 54 min.

Times are pure reading, without breaks. At 300 words per page, this edition would be about 233 pages.
