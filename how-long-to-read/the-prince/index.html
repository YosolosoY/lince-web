How long does it take to read The Prince?

The Prince by Niccolò Machiavelli has about 45,150 words in the edition included in Lince. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, that is about 3 h 10 min.

Times are pure reading, without breaks. At 300 words per page, this edition would be about 150 pages.
