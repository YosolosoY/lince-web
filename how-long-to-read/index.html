How long does it take to read a book?

An average adult reads a 300-page book in about 6 hours. For the classics below we counted the real words, so you can see the time at your own speed.

How we calculate it

Time is the number of words divided by your words per minute. We start at 238 words per minute, the estimate for silent reading of non-fiction in English from a review of 190 studies. Fiction averaged about 260, and your real speed changes with the book and how tired you are.

Why pages are only an estimate

A page can hold anywhere from 200 to 400 words depending on the edition, so we count 300. If you know a book's word count, that gives a better answer.
