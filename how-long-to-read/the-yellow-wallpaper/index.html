How long does it take to read The Yellow Wallpaper?

The Yellow Wallpaper by Charlotte Perkins Gilman has about 6,078 words in the edition included in Lince. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, that is about 26 min.

Reading time at different speeds

Times are pure reading, without breaks. At 300 words per page, this edition would be about 20 pages.
