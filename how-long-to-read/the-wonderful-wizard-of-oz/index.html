How long does it take to read The Wonderful Wizard of Oz?

The Wonderful Wizard of Oz by L. Frank Baum has about 39,272 words in the edition included in Lince. At the average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, that is about 2 h 45 min.

Reading time at different speeds

Times are pure reading, without breaks. At 300 words per page, this edition would be about 131 pages.
