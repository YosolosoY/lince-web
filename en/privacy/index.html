Lince

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · Versión en español

In short: what you read in Lince stays on your phone. We show no ads, sell no data and don't track what you do. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store only your email, your name and how many files you have imported, so the free plan limits work with your account.

Who is responsible

Lince is an app developed by Mateo Monsalve (Chile). For any privacy question, write to mateo.monsalve@gmail.com.

What is stored only on your phone

All of this stays on your device and is never sent to any server:

  • The texts and books you paste or import (.txt, .epub, .pdf), and the Classic Library books you pick.
  • Your progress in each text, your reading sessions, streak, points (XP), badges and records.
  • Your answers to "How much did you understand?" and your speed test results.
  • Your settings: language, theme, colors, font, speed, daily goal and reminder time.
  • The addresses and, if you enter them, the username and password of the OPDS libraries you add.

If you uninstall the app, all of this is deleted from your phone.

If you sign in with Google (optional)

You can use Lince without an account. We only ask you to sign in with Google to import files or to buy Lince Plus. If you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, your name and your Google account identifier.
  • A record of your imports: the date and number of words of each imported file (not its content or file name), to apply the free import limit.

This data is stored with Supabase, our account provider, on servers located in São Paulo, Brazil. It is only used to run the app; it is never sold or shared for advertising.

Purchases

Lince Plus purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. To know whether you have Plus we use RevenueCat, which receives the purchase information from Google Play (product, date and subscription status) linked to an anonymous identifier of your Lince account.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't access your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We don't upload the texts you read.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, your own phone schedules it. It doesn't go through any server.

How to delete your data

  • Data on your phone: delete a text by pressing and holding it in your library, or uninstall the app to delete everything.
  • Your account: in the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete my account. Your account and your import record are deleted immediately. You can also request it by email; see how to delete your account.

Children

Lince is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is important, we will also announce it in the app.
