Delete my Lince account

Lince (Android app) · Versión en español

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Lince and tap the Settings gear.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to lince.revision@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Lince account", from the same Google email you signed in with. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name and Google identifier.
  • Your import record (dates and number of words).

We don't store any other data about you on our servers. What is on your phone (texts, progress, streak, settings) is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What is kept

If you bought Lince Plus, Google Play keeps the purchase record under its own policies, and RevenueCat keeps the transaction history for as long as the law requires (for example, for refunds or tax purposes). To cancel a subscription, do it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
